# Brain Trust cube and cosmos

Six native tabs: Lab, Chat Room, Tournament, Comedy Club / Laughly, Mission Control, and Audio. Existing authentication, provider calls, owner proposal controls, scoring, and voice tools are retained.

The menu skin takes its direction from Xbox 360-era Halo: translucent cobalt layers, a warm selected-row highlight, larger angular lettering, a vertical sector list, and subtle synthesized selection tones. No Halo logos, screenshots, game audio, or other game assets are distributed. Oxanium is bundled with its SIL Open Font License.

The cube rotates to a selected face and travels toward the fixed camera over 980 ms, with delayed light echoes. The matching menu enters through an optical flash and staggered controls; returning reverses the flight. Reduced-motion preferences suppress decorative animation.

The default sky is Webb's NGC 346 MIRI observation. Cube mode uses a subdued cool blue display tint. Astronomy mode restores the observation's published representative colors and shrinks the cube into an instrument position. Next observation switches between NGC 346 and M74. Images are published observations, with their release dates, instrument, source, and credits shown; this is an archive gallery.
